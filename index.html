Present Simple

Timetables and schedules (trains, buses, lessons, films). e.g. The train leaves at 8:00.

Present Continuous

Fixed plans with a time or place. e.g. I am meeting Anna at 5:00.

be going to

Personal intentions/plans and predictions you can see. e.g. Look at the clouds! It is going to rain.

will

Quick decisions, promises, offers, and opinions. e.g. I will help you with that bag!

Creative Writing: My Next Weekend / Holiday Plans

Try to use at least 3 different future forms:

  • • Present Simple for a timetable (a train, bus, flight, or movie time).
  • • Present Continuous for a fixed plan with someone.
  • • be going to for your personal plan or intention.
  • • will for a hope, promise, or opinion (e.g. "I think it will be fun!").
