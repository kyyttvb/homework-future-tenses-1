1. Present Simple

Use for timetables & schedules (trains, buses, flights, lessons, movies).

"The train leaves at 8:00."

2. Present Continuous

Use for fixed arrangements with a specific time or place (already organized).

"I am meeting Anna tonight."

3. be going to

Use for personal plans & intentions (and predictions with clear evidence).

"I am going to buy a new phone."

4. will

Use for instant decisions, promises, offers, and predictions (often with I think).

"I think it will rain."
